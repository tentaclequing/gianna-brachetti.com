## AI governance

Between roles after four years at [DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/) (2022-2026), where I owned organic growth and became the cross-functional lead for AI governance: risk assessment, policy, and compliance for AI systems across 30+ language markets. Now focused on bringing that experience to the public sector, where the stakes are highest and the frameworks are still being built.

## Building

Running a self-hosted AI stack on a local server: [Open Web UI](https://openwebui.com/) as the interface, [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) for local inference, and a European model provider for larger models, all routed exclusively through EU-hosted infrastructure. Built a knowledge base pipeline on top of it for AI governance research and domain-specific retrieval. On the coding side, running [OpenCode](https://opencode.ai/) as an agentic coding environment with the same EU-only model routing, custom MCP servers for knowledge search and browser rendering, and a sandboxed execution environment that keeps AI tooling isolated from the rest of the system.

On the hardware side: a [GrapheneOS](https://grapheneos.org/) phone paired with a [Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org/) LoRa device for off-grid mesh communication, independent of mobile networks or internet access. Still working through the growing pains of getting the right hardware variant for the firmware I actually need.

Ironically, the one thing I cannot get to work is updating my printer's firmware (le big sigh). Why do printers have to be so mean.
